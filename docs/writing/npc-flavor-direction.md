# NPC flavor direction (READ BEFORE ANY FILL PASS)

Standing creative direction from the author for reflavoring generic NPC dialogue
around the gym leaders (and everyone). This overrides the "fill list" angles where
they conflict. Applies to ALL leaders, not just Surge.

## The core doctrine: slight nudges, deep, mystique

- NPC lines are **slight nudges**. Subtle, vague, oblique. They gesture at the
  leader's character; they never explain it.
- **SHOW, NEVER TELL.** Present a concrete small detail, an image, an unfinished
  thought. Never state a verdict about the leader (good/great/strong/kind). Let the
  PLAYER supply the emotion. The meaning is for the player to build, not for the NPC
  to hand over.
- **Keep the mystique.** Leave gaps. Trail off. Do not resolve. A line that answers
  the question is worse than a line that deepens it.
- **Respect the reader.** The player is intelligent. Do not push a direction, moralize,
  or spell out the takeaway. That is the shallow register and it is banned here.

## Banned (this is the shallow version - do NOT do it)

- Cheerleading / hype: "he's the best," "everyone loves X," "the strongest," "so good."
- "Ask the LEADER" / "ask him" / directing the player to the leader for answers.
- The leader praising themselves, or NPCs saying the leader "knows how good he is."
- Social-media voice: punchy, on-the-nose, applause-lines, everyone-agrees consensus.
- Exposition dumps that state the arc outright.
- The same beat repeated across NPCs (a chorus all parroting one fact).

## Do instead

- Concrete over abstract. A specific object, a small remembered act, a habit, a silence.
- Understatement. The bigger the feeling, the plainer the words.
- Ambiguity and restraint. Say less. Let one detail imply the rest.
- Vary the angle per NPC so no two lines lean on the same idea.

### Before / after (Surge: quiet, acts instead of talks, a good soul)

- BAD (shallow, tell): "The LT's all thunder till it counts. Then you see why we
  sleep easy here." -> states the verdict, cheerleads.
- BAD (shallow): "This whole city dotes on electric. There's a reason. Ask the LT."
  -> hype + "ask him."
- GOOD (nudge, show): "Storm took the docks two winters back. Someone had the lamps
  back on by morning. Nobody saw who." -> a deed, no name, no verdict; player infers.
- GOOD (nudge): "He fixed my boat's wiring one night. Wouldn't take a coin. Said
  maybe four words the whole time." -> character through a small action, understated.
- GOOD (nudge): "Quiet man. You notice the quiet ones only by what stops going wrong."

## Per-leader voice notes

Draw the SUBSTANCE from each leader's docs/writing/drafts/<leader>.md + lore-ledger,
but always render it as a slight nudge per the doctrine above.

- **Surge** - quiet; acts, does not talk; a good soul; earned calm. NPCs know his
  deeds by their results, never by his words. Never have anyone quote him at length

  the punchline. A detail (a ringing phone, a monthly errand) the player connects,

- **Misty** - her solitude, the duck, only-what-can't-leave-the-water: shown by

## Cross-nudges (foreshadowing other characters)

Same rules, more so. A cross-nudge is a faint echo (a faceless man, an absence, a
distant storm), never a signpost. If it reads as foreshadowing, it is too loud.

## Process

- Every fill pass reads THIS file first. Lines that state a verdict or cheerlead get
  rejected and rewritten before commit.
- When in doubt, write less and leave more unsaid.
