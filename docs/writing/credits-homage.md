# In-game credits: homage + disclaimer block

Draft copy for the end-game credits roll. Short lines (the credits scroller is
narrow, roughly 24 characters). No em dashes. Keep it warm and plain.

Wiring note: this is TEXT ONLY. To show it in-game it must be added to the
credits sequence in `src/credits.c` (the credits string tables). That is a
separate code step - say the word and I will wire it in.

---

## Block copy (as it should read on screen)

```
FAIR RED

an unofficial, non-commercial
fan project

- RIVAL TRIBUTES -

BUHRITO
a fond parody of a creator
the author admires

TWITCH
a tribute to TWITCH PLAYS
POKéMON and its chat

their lines are lovingly
adapted from the creators'
own words and streams

made as a fan, with respect

not affiliated with, endorsed
by, or associated with these
creators or any platform

if a referenced creator
objects, it comes down

contact: <your contact here>
```

## Notes

- Replace `<your contact here>` with a real channel you check (email or a
  handle). A reachable contact is the single most important line for good faith.
- If you would rather NOT state that the lines are adapted from their own words,
  delete the "their lines are lovingly adapted..." stanza. Recommendation: keep
  it. It reads as credit, not lifting.
- "TWITCH" here points at Twitch Plays Pokemon (the 2014 crowd phenomenon and its
  memes: Bird Jesus, Lord Helix). The "or any platform" line covers the platform
  trademark concern by disclaiming association.
