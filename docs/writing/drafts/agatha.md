# Agatha - line bank (in-game)

> Elite Four arc (Ghost). Index/gates in ../lore-ledger.md (AGATHA-*). Gen-3
> rhythm: short two-line pages. No em dashes. Agatha is the SWEET-OLD-LADY-WITH-BITE:
> acid tongue, terrifying temper, but genuinely, warmly on the side of any trainer
> who battles to prove herself. Her comedy is WIT and barbs, never slapstick.

## Concept / engine (REALIGNED: study vs. battle)
- SPINE = the schism with OAK. Decades ago Agatha and a young OAK were the two
  fiercest trainers in Kanto. Then OAK laid down his team to catalogue POKéMON in a
  book. To Agatha that was surrender: you do not prove a POKéMON's worth by writing
  it down, you prove it in a battle. Her vanilla intro IS this thesis, verbatim:
  "He's wrong. POKéMON are for battling!"
- She stayed in the arena OAK left, and kept winning, until she became the oldest
  ELITE FOUR member who ever lived, as if daring age to make her quit the way he
  quit. Her GHOST POKéMON and acid tongue are the armor of the last one standing.
- THE WARMTH IS MOTIVATED. Agatha has no patience for POKéDEX-fillers and
  catch-everything busywork. But a trainer who fights to prove herself, who chases
  strength instead of pages, is exactly what OAK stopped being. When the player
  turns out to be that trainer, Agatha lights up: she calls you "child," tells you
  to live long, and means it. She sees a kindred spirit, and maybe the successor
  OAK never made.
- COY UNDERLAYER (kept, demoted from the spine): whether she and OAK were ever more
  than rivals. NEVER answered. A photo of two young trainers, OAK going quiet at her
  name, "we were young." The grief is real but it is the hidden layer now; the
  study-vs-battle rift is the surface the player actually reads.
