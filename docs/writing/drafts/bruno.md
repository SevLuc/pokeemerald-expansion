> Elite Four arc (Fighting). Index/gates in ../lore-ledger.md (BRUNO-*). Gen-3
> rhythm: short two-line pages. No em dashes. Bruno is the roster's SINCERE
> ANCHOR: zero gags, plays fully straight so the fight lands. His whole method is
> SHOW, NEVER TELL, so NPCs report what they SAW, never his philosophy.

## Concept / engine
- The gentle giant in a permanent state of calm. Stand near Bruno and your nerves
  settle. That stillness is the root of his strength: no ego, no anger, no noise,
  so he can pour everything into pure power.
- He NEVER explains himself. His demeanor is the whole message. Humble, patient,
  lives up in the wild among free POKéMON that do not fear him, and quietly tends
  the land he moves through.
- The one thing that draws him down from the quiet is the pure joy of meeting
  someone truly strong (canon: he joined the ELITE FOUR "out of his burning
  ambition to battle the best"). His gracious defeat line encodes this: losing to
  a worthy challenger is exactly what he came for.
- Mystique rule (his is the strictest): NEVER state "calm is his strength" or
  "he is at peace." NPCs describe the ease they felt near him, the calm wild
  POKéMON, the cleaned trail, the power that followed the peace. The player links
  calm -> strength. His power is only ever implied ALONGSIDE his calm.

## Battle voice (LOCKED: revised, closer = Option 2)
Vanilla Bruno boasts ("We will grind you down with our superior power! Hoo hah!")
and his defeat is ego-shock ("Why? How could I lose?"). Both are off-brand for the
zen giant, so both are replaced. Warm and at-ease on the way in, gracious on the
loss, respectful on the way out.
