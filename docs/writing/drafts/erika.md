# Erika - line bank (in-game)

> Celadon arc. Index/gates in ../lore-ledger.md (ERIKA-01..). Gen-3 rhythm: short
> two-line pages. No em dashes. MYSTIQUE BY DESIGN: no plain telling, ever. Every
> line is a coy, contradictory rumor. The player never learns the truth.

## Concept / engine
- SURFACE (canon, untouched): the gentle "Nature-Loving Princess" who keeps
  dozing off mid-sentence. Her own gym lines stay vanilla-archetypal.
- UNDER IT: nobody actually knows. The town trades five contradictory legends,
  each believed by a different kind of person, and none is ever confirmed. She
  likes it that way.
- ANCHOR (the one agreed fact): she is never seen awake, and her garden grows
  flowers found nowhere else in Kanto. Every rumor hangs off this.
- CONTRAST (silly-through-contrast): a menacing Rocket grunt spooked by a florist;
  a dozing woman who gave an old man lifelong insomnia; a leader everyone reveres
  and no one knows.

## The five facets (each a coy rumor, mutually contradictory)
1. Poison/medicine master. Her flowers/perfumes heal or harm; "never sniff one
   twice." (Little Girl, ERIKA-01)
2. Her perfume keeps the city serene/beautiful, on the wind. (Woman, ERIKA-02)
3. She was once a fearsome battler. (Fat Man, ERIKA-03)
4. The "ladies only" gym is a refuge/school; "she teaches them more than
   flowers." (Old Man 1, ERIKA-04)
5. Even Team Rocket steers clear of her garden, "Boss's orders." (Rocket Grunt,
   ERIKA-05) - CANON-SAFE: they avoid her; it never says she fights them.

## Payoff = the refusal to answer
Gym trainer Lisa (post-battle) gathers it and settles nothing: "Ask ten of us,
you'll get ten ERIKAs. …She likes it that way." (ERIKA-PAYOFF)
Post-badge wink, Old Man 1 gated on FLAG_BADGE04_GET: "You beat ERIKA? And you
still can't tell me one true thing about her. …That's ERIKA." (ERIKA-08)

## Two gym/town gimmicks (concrete gags; Erika herself stays unknowable)
1. THE BIKERS WHO FLED (Route 16). The Cycling-Road bikers west of Celadon
   abandoned the city in terror of the perfume/medicine master and now camp on the
   route with their beloved POISON types. Light running gag: they dodge Celadon
   taxes. (Lao, Ruben, Hideo, Camron.) They also reinforce the poison legend from
   the outside. See ERIKA-BIKERS.
2. BROCK'S EXES TAKE REFUGE (Celadon Gym). The "ladies only" gym is where Brock's
   "previous sponsors" end up; Erika hosts them all and is NOT one herself. COY per
   the Brock arc: "PEWTER number," "let it ring," "generous every single month."
   (Kay, Lori, Mary.) Cross-links the BROCK arc. See ERIKA-EXES.
