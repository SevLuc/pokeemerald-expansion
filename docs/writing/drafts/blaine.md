# Blaine - line bank (in-game)

> Cinnabar arc. Index/gates in ../lore-ledger.md (BLAINE-*). Gen-3 rhythm: short
> two-line pages. No em dashes. Guilt behind the bluster; the fight stays red-hot.

## Concept / engine
- SURFACE (canon): the red-hot, boisterous QUIZ-MASTER of fire.
- HIDDEN: he was a lead researcher at the Cinnabar POKéMON MANSION, on DR. FUJI's
  team. They cloned MEW and pushed too far, making MEWTWO. It broke loose, burned
  the mansion (and Blaine), and vanished. The fire persona IS the fire he unleashed.
- THE QUIZ, reframed: raw power without judgment made a monster, so he tests the
  MIND before the POKéMON. Wisdom before power. His penance and his lesson.
- CANON-BLEND: Blaine-on-Fuji's-team is Adventures-manga flavor, supported by the
  in-repo "photo of BLAINE and MR. FUJI" line and the townsfolk saying he predates
  the LAB. Contradicts no game plot. Tag canon-check.

## Delivery
- BLAINE himself: quiz-as-wisdom reframe in the intro; the guilt leaks in the
  quieter beats (defeat "I have watched that happen before, to everything I built";
  badge "I did not, once"; TM "it can burn more than you aim it at").
- The MANSION journals (anchor): coy signed fragments beside the canon MEWTWO diary
  - 3F wonder ("he wept. So did I. -F."), B1F confession ("No one asked if we
  should. Forgive me. -B.").
- The LAB (payoff, plain telling): the Entrance scientist ("FUJI's sharpest… the old
  MANSION team… never touched science again, only questions now") + the Fuji photo
  ("beside him, younger, unburned: BLAINE. Two friends who made something they could
  not unmake").
- Cinnabar townsfolk (coy): Woman (he was a brilliant scientist, "does not speak of
  then"), Old Man ("some say he lit it. …Some say he just could not put it out").
