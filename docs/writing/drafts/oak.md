# Professor Oak - line bank (in-game)

> Central character. Index/gates in ../lore-ledger.md (OAK-*). Gen-3 rhythm: short
> two-line pages. No em dashes. Oak is the DATA-HUNGRY GOOFBALL: the revered elder
> statesman of POKéMON who is, underneath, a completionist gremlin. Full comedy
> from his FIRST in-world line (Route 1), escalating to mania at the DEX charge.

## Concept / engine
- Kanto reveres Professor Oak. Oak reveres DATA. His life's dream is to study every
  POKéMON that exists, and he has cheerfully decided the player is the instrument of
  that dream: a one-person catching machine who will fill his POKéDEX to the last
  entry.
- Warm, brilliant, tangent-prone, and completely unable to imagine why anyone would
  catch LESS than everything. He treats catching as science and science as the
  greatest adventure ever undertaken.
- Trainers who deliberately limit themselves (keep one POKéMON per route, retire the
  fallen) leave him baffled and comically IN DENIAL: surely the player won't do that
  to him. Surely you'll catch them all. …You will, won't you?
- Silly-through-contrast: the dignified mentor is a data gremlin. His aides across
  Kanto are a long-suffering chorus for a man who will not stop phoning them.
