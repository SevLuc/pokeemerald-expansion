## Concept / engine
- SURFACE (canon, untouched): the terrifying, theatrical "Poisonous Ninja Master."
  His battle intro/defeat stay archetypal; the CEO only leaks after you beat him.
- HIDDEN: he is the extravagant CEO of KOGA INDUSTRIES, Kanto's POWER/ENERGY
  utility. His poison-gas POKéMON run the refineries; the smog over Fuchsia is the
  price of the region's electricity.
- NECESSARY EVIL: the nature town + Safari Zone resent the pollution, but everyone
  depends on his power. He even funds half the Safari preserve. Not a villain.
- TWO DEEPER LAYERS (gym insiders reveal): (a) the invisible-wall gym is a
  PROTESTER-REPELLENT; (b) he is a master SPIN DOCTOR - costume + gimmick gym =
  deliberate misdirection. He sincerely believes he is the hero of his story.
- CONTRAST: a pantomime ninja who is quietly a polluting power baron; the "creeping
  horror of poison" is literally industrial smog.
