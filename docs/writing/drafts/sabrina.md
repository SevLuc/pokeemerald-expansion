## Concept / engine
- Sabrina was a sunny, laughing child. Her psychic gift grew past her; to cage it
  she let her heart go cold. The cold thing she became REACHES: she folds SAFFRON's
  minds into her own; the vanished "sleep" inside her and in her dolls.
- PIVOT (adapted from the anime): she has not felt anything in years. Losing to the
  player makes her LAUGH for the first time in ages. The laugh cracks the seal, the
  cold lets go, the vanished wake unharmed with NO memory of the dark, no grudges.
  She is the sunny girl again.
- The Marsh Badge mechanic (over-level mons disobey) ties to her: "Power without a
  warm hand goes cold. …No one knows that better."

## Gym gimmick (possession)
- Everyone inside is possessed and forced to fight. Each puppet gives a DIFFERENT
  false direction to reach her: Amanda=north, Johan=south, Stacy=left, Tyron=right,
  Tasha=stand still, Cameron=center panel, Preston=turn back. Contradictory on
  purpose, the hints are useless.
- Each DEFEAT is a brief lucid flicker ("that was not me"; "believe nothing we tell
  you"; "keep going, reach her").
- Gym Guide is possessed too but warns: "Trust NO ONE in this gym. …Not even me,"
  and lets one truth slip (FIGHTING folds fast to PSYCHIC).
