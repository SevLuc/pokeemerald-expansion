## About the characters

Fair Red is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project, built from source on top
of the open-source pokeemerald-expansion. It is free, contains no commercial
assets, and is not for sale.

Two of the game's rivals are affectionate tributes to creators and communities
the author genuinely admires:

- **Buhrito** is a fond parody inspired by a streamer the author is a fan of.
- **Twitch** is a tribute to **Twitch Plays Pokémon**, the 2014 crowd-played
  phenomenon, and its chat culture (Bird Jesus, Lord Helix, and the rest).

Their dialogue is lovingly adapted from the creators' own words, catchphrases,
and streams. The names are kept on purpose: the whole point is to celebrate
these creators and send curious players their way. This is meant as a love
letter, not a lift.
